## 1. Introduction
Mukhtar Ahmed Printing & Computer Store ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Mukhtar Printing & Store"), which provides printing services, office supplies, flex banners, stamps, and computer accessories in Umarzai and surrounding areas in Pakistan.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum personal data required to fulfill orders:
- **Contact & Delivery Information**: Full name, mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact number (optional), and physical delivery address within Umarzai / Charsadda.
- **Uploaded Documents & Customer Artwork**: Digital files (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX up to 25 MB) provided voluntarily by you for flex printing, posters, photocopy, composing, or business card fabrication.
- **Order & Transaction Records**: Itemized cart contents, custom printing dimensions/quantities, selected payment method (Cash on Delivery, Shop Pickup, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or HBL Bank Transfer), and order timestamps.
- **Device & Technical Information**: Anonymous error logs, device model, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to send push notifications regarding order progress.

## 3. How We Use Collected Data
The information we collect is strictly utilized to:
1. Process, produce, and dispatch customer printing jobs and merchandise.
2. Calculate accurate custom dimensions and pricing (e.g. flex banner square-footage).
3. Contact customers for proof validation, design review, or collection notifications.
4. Provide customer support via telephone or official WhatsApp chat (`03149958963`).
5. Transmit push notifications as orders move through stages: Confirmed, Designing, Printing, Ready for Pickup, and Completed.

## 4. File Upload & Storage Security
Customer-uploaded files are transmitted via TLS/HTTPS encryption and stored in private Google Firebase Storage directories scoped to the customer's authenticated user ID (`/orders/{userId}/{fileName}`). Uploaded files are:
- Accessible solely by the customer and authorized shop production personnel (Mukhtar Ahmed).
- Retained only as long as needed for production fulfillment and repeat re-order convenience.
- Never sold, licensed, or shared with third-party advertisers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

## 6. Data Security & Encryption
All communication between the mobile app and backend services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+). Customer data stored at rest in Google Cloud / Firebase utilizes industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

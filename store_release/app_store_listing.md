```text
Mukhtar Printing & Store is the premier mobile app for printing supplies, document services, and custom banner production in Umarzai, Charsadda, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Operated by Mukhtar Ahmed Printing & Computer Store at Main Bazar Umarzai, our app brings our print shop directly to your iPhone.

FEATURES:
• Shop Printing Supplies: Buy genuine HP and Canon printer toner cartridges, ink refill bottles, premium A4 paper reams, glossy photo sheets, and PVC cards.
• 25+ Printing & Design Services: Order panaflex banners, visiting cards, wedding invitation cards, photostate copies, document binding, rubber stamps, and certificate printing.
• Live Price Calculator: Calculate your exact printing costs instantly using custom banner dimensions (width × height) or document page counts.
• Fast File Uploads: Attach design files, photos, or PDF documents up to 25 MB directly from your device.
• Live Order Tracking: Watch your order progress through 6 stages: Placed, Confirmed, Designing, Printing, Ready for Pickup, and Completed.
• Easy Payment Options: Pay with Cash on Delivery, in-store pickup payment, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or direct bank transfer.
• Direct Shop Support: Connect directly with shop owner Mukhtar Ahmed via phone call or WhatsApp with one tap.

Serving students, offices, and businesses in Umarzai, Charsadda, and surrounding areas.
```
